# AI Services Workspace (`ai-services/`)

## Purpose
The `ai-services/` directory isolates the artificial intelligence engines, quantitative trading algorithms, LLM agent orchestrations, and market intelligence models powering AI TradeQ.

## Responsibilities
- Autonomous market strategy generation and execution models.
- Sentiment analysis, financial news processing, and market signal extraction.
- Multi-agent workflow execution and prompt management.
- Risk management modeling and predictive analytics.
