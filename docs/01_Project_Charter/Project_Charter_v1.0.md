## 1. Executive Summary

AI TradeQ is an enterprise-grade AI-powered Crypto Market Research and Decision Intelligence Platform designed to help users make better-informed trading decisions through evidence-based analysis.

The platform combines live market data, technical analysis, sentiment intelligence, on-chain analytics, macroeconomic context, and AI reasoning into a unified experience. Every recommendation will include supporting evidence and a confidence assessment rather than presenting guaranteed outcomes.

This Project Charter defines the project's purpose, objectives, governance, scope, stakeholders, success criteria, and operating principles.

---

## 2. Project Vision

Build an enterprise-grade AI platform that performs professional-quality crypto market research using multiple AI agents and trusted data sources, enabling users to make informed trading decisions through transparent and explainable analysis.

---

## 3. Mission Statement

Develop an intelligent research platform that continuously collects, analyzes, and explains market information so users can evaluate opportunities and risks with greater confidence.

---

## 4. Business Objectives

### Primary Objectives
- Build an AI-powered crypto research platform.
- Provide explainable market analysis.
- Reduce research time for traders.
- Deliver institutional-quality reporting.
- Support multiple cryptocurrencies.

### Long-Term Objectives
- Expand beyond crypto into other financial markets.
- Offer enterprise SaaS capabilities.
- Support team collaboration and API integrations.
- Build a scalable AI product ecosystem.

---

## 5. Project Scope

### In Scope
- AI-powered market analysis
- Multi-agent reasoning
- Real-time market intelligence
- Technical indicator analysis
- Sentiment analysis
- On-chain analytics (where supported)
- Portfolio monitoring
- Alerts and notifications
- Explainable reports
- Web dashboard
- Secure user management

### Out of Scope (Version 1)
- Automated trade execution
- Financial guarantees
- High-frequency trading
- Custody of user assets
- Brokerage services

## 7. Project Principles

The project will follow these principles:

1. **Documentation First**
2. **Evidence Before Conclusions**
3. **Security by Design**
4. **Explainable AI**
5. **Modular Architecture**
6. **API-First Design**
7. **Scalability by Default**
8. **Continuous Improvement**

---

## 8. Governance Model

Every feature must follow the same lifecycle:

1. Requirement
2. Design
3. Documentation Review
4. Approval
5. Implementation
6. Testing
7. Audit
8. Fixes
9. Final Approval

> [!IMPORTANT]
> No implementation may begin without approved documentation.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when it:

- Produces reliable, evidence-based market analysis.
- Explains recommendations clearly.
- Responds within defined performance targets.
- Provides a scalable and maintainable architecture.
- Meets approved functional and non-functional requirements.

---

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Market volatility** | Probability-based recommendations with clear reasoning |
| **External API failures** | Multiple providers and graceful fallbacks |
| **AI hallucinations** | Tool-based retrieval, validation, and structured reasoning |
| **Scope expansion** | Controlled change management and phased roadmap |

---

## 11. Assumptions

- Reliable market data providers will be available.
- AI models and external services will be accessible within operational constraints.
- Documentation will remain the authoritative source for implementation.
- Stakeholders will review and approve major documents before development.

---

## 12. Constraints

- Development will proceed in phased releases.
- Features will be prioritized based on approved roadmap.
- All recommendations must avoid presenting guaranteed financial outcomes.
- Security and performance requirements apply to every release.
